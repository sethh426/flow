# Flow sales operating rules
Updated October 14, 2026. Owner direction: build useful products people can try free; demonstrate value before asking for a discovery conversation.

## Persistent working agreement
- Lead with a relevant working workflow and its actual output. Do not make a conversation a prerequisite for receiving value.
- Build ten usable free workflow plugins as the first catalog. Free local tools need no account or billable API calls. State the boundary before the user runs them.
- Match outreach to the recipient's published process. Explain one practical use; never turn plausible fit into a claim of confirmed pain.
- After verification, offer the exact tool, a real example and one low-effort next action. Avoid generic questions asking the buyer to invent our use case.
- Show the work before claiming the result. No invented savings, testimonials, customer references or operational integrations.
- Offer separately scoped paid configuration, connected execution or support when it adds value. Free access must not imply hidden free provider usage or unlimited custom implementation.
- Preserve consent, prior-contact checks, suppression, sender identity and full-message verification. This direction does not authorize automatic replies or bypass previous outreach protections.
- Measure successful first use, repeat use, requests for help, paid scopes and actual receipts. Reply counts alone are not product validation; downloads alone are not revenue.
- After a batch, classify failures: contact failure, no relevant use, unclear offer, first-run failure, no repeat value, or no reason to upgrade. Change one variable at a time and record evidence. Do not automatically scale a weak batch.

## Research applied, not claimed mastery
Reviewed October 14, 2026:
- Salesforce, Value Selling: https://www.salesforce.com/blog/sales/value-selling/ — connect an offer to the customer's actual needs and substantiate value.
- Salesforce, Outcome Based Selling: https://www.salesforce.com/blog/sales/outcome-based-selling/ — evaluate usefulness through the customer's desired outcome.
- HubSpot free tools catalog: https://legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-product-and-services-catalog/free-tools — example of bounded free access alongside commercial tiers; not proof that Flow's offer will convert.

Working experiment: a ready-to-use local workflow with transparent limits may lower first-use friction compared with an abstract custom-workflow pitch. Measure this; do not call it proven.

The repository is the durable project reference. These rules are not a claim that the assistant has permanently retrained itself or completed a sales certification.
